Codex 001 · The First Observation

The Six Were Never One

The first archivist made a mistake: they searched for one master ledger. They found six systems with different rules, different designs, and different ways of reaching agreement. The mistake was believing that connection required sameness.

Ethereum remembers through a shared consensus. Base extends Ethereum through a rollup design. Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum-compatible layer-two network built on Arbitrum technology. BNB Chain has its own validator and execution design. Solana uses a distinctive time-ordering mechanism. Sui organizes state around programmable objects.

“A key is not a kingdom. A bridge is not a border erased.”
Codex 002 · The Watchers

Why the Omnis Were Sent

The watchers were small enough to pass unnoticed and patient enough to wait through ages of blocks. Each carried a different mark, but none carried the entire message. Their task was not to conquer a realm or declare one chain superior.

They were sent to learn the rules of each world. Only then could they recognize what traveled between worlds — and what could never travel without help.

“Collect the fragments. Question the record. Never mistake a rumor for a proof.”

Chapter I · Ethereum

The Witness Without a Pen

SEALED

The Flame does not ask every witness to rewrite the whole history. Instead, a validator signs a statement supporting information it has observed about the chain's state or progress. These statements help the network reach agreement.

“I am not the block, nor the hand that creates it. I am a signed declaration that says: I have seen this, and I stand behind this view. What am I?”

Reveal a hint

Ethereum validators make these statements as part of proof-of-stake consensus. The word begins with A.

ARCHIVE TRUTH: An attestation is a validator's signed statement. Ethereum uses proof of stake; validators propose blocks and attest to chain checkpoints as part of the consensus process.

Chapter II · Base

The Road Beyond the Walls

SEALED

The Blue Passage grew because the main road could not carry every traveler cheaply on its own. A second system executes activity, then posts data or commitments so the parent chain can help secure the result. The passage is connected to Ethereum, but it is not Ethereum's base layer.

“I gather many journeys away from the crowded road, then carry evidence back to the chain that anchors my security. I am not a bridge, and I am not the base layer. What kind of system am I?”

Reveal a hint

Base is built as an Ethereum layer two using OP Stack technology. The answer is a general type of scaling system.

ARCHIVE TRUTH: A rollup executes transactions on a layer two and posts data or commitments to a layer one. Base is an Ethereum L2 built using the OP Stack. Rollup designs differ in how they establish validity and handle data.

Chapter III · Robinhood Chain

The Toll at the New Gate

SEALED

The Green Threshold is a newer frontier, yet its foundations are familiar to Ethereum builders. The network is designed as an Ethereum-compatible layer two built on Arbitrum technology. Even a new frontier must account for the cost of computation.

“I am not the name of the realm, nor the token that marks its doorway. I pay for the work of execution here, as I do on Ethereum's main network. Speak my three-letter ticker.”

Reveal a hint

Look for the native gas asset documented for Robinhood Chain. It is the same asset used for gas on Ethereum mainnet.

ARCHIVE TRUTH: ETH is used to pay gas on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum-compatible layer two built on Arbitrum technology. Always check current official documentation before sending assets or interacting with a new network.

Chapter IV · BNB Chain

The Coin That Opens the Index

SEALED

The Golden Index is not opened by a title, a badge, or a promise. Transactions consume computational resources, and the network requires payment for that work. The realm's native token serves as the usual gas asset on BNB Smart Chain.

“Three letters name the asset that pays the toll on this road. It is also the name of the wider chain family. What mark appears on the gas ledger?”

Reveal a hint

The ticker and the token name are the same: B-N-B.

ARCHIVE TRUTH: BNB pays gas fees on BNB Smart Chain. Gas accounts for the computational work required by transactions and smart contracts. Token roles can differ across networks in the broader BNB Chain ecosystem.

Chapter V · Solana

The Clock That Is Not a Judge

SEALED

The Shifting Current moves quickly, but speed alone cannot establish agreement. Solana uses a cryptographic mechanism that helps establish verifiable passage of time and order between events. It works with consensus; it is not, by itself, the consensus mechanism.

“I leave a verifiable sequence, like a clock whose ticks can be checked after the fact. I help order events, but I do not decide alone which history the network accepts. What am I called?”

Reveal a hint

The name contains four words. It starts with “Proof of” and ends with “History.”

ARCHIVE TRUTH: Proof of History (PoH) is a cryptographic clock/order mechanism that helps establish the passage of time between events. It is used alongside Solana's consensus design; PoH alone is not the network's consensus mechanism.

Chapter VI · Sui

The Thing That Is More Than a Row

SEALED

The Unwritten does not describe every asset as a mere row in a universal account table. Sui's object-centric model represents assets and other pieces of state as typed objects, with ownership and rules that determine how they can be used.

“I may be owned, transferred, or changed according to my rules. My type tells you what I am, and my identity distinguishes me from another of my kind. In this realm, what is the fundamental unit?”

Reveal a hint

The answer is a common word for a thing, but in Sui it has a specific technical role.

ARCHIVE TRUTH: An object is a core unit of state in Sui. Objects have types and ownership rules. Different ownership and transaction patterns affect how the network processes them.

Final chapter · The Omni Archive

The Passage Between Worlds

LOCKED

Six fragments remain scattered. The Master Trial will open when all six realm records have been recovered.

“I do not make six ledgers become one. I do not erase their different rules. I carry supported assets or messages between separate networks, relying on mechanisms that must be trusted or verified. What am I?”

Reveal a hint

It is a common cross-chain tool. The word suggests a way to cross a gap.

MASTER RECORD RECOVERED: A bridge can transfer supported assets or messages between networks. Bridge designs vary in their security assumptions; bridging does not merge blockchains into one ledger. The six realms remain distinct, and that is the secret: connection is not sameness.
