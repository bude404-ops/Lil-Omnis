The permanent record

What you discover stays with you

Once an achievement is earned and verified, it will remain in the archive record for the wallet that earned it—even if the qualifying NFTs are later sold or transferred.

Lore discoveries and completed story milestones will remain recorded as well. Permanent achievements do not automatically guarantee eligibility for future holder rewards.

Future distributions

Airdrops and holder rewards

Future airdrops, digital items, and holder-only benefits may use separate eligibility rules. Each distribution should clearly state its qualifying collections, chains, snapshot date or block, and any other requirements.

Earning an achievement does not guarantee that a future airdrop or paid reward will be offered.

The current status

This page introduces the planned achievement system. Wallet connection, blockchain ownership checks, bridge verification, and permanent achievement records are not active yet.

Wallet safety

Never enter your seed phrase or private key into this website. Future access should use a wallet connection and, where appropriate, a clearly explained signature.
