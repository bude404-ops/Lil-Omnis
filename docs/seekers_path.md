# The Seeker's Path — progression & unlock design

> "They do not simply collect. They remember." — Codex Fragment 001

The Seeker's Path is Lil Omnis' depth system: the more you hold and the more you
**travel your pieces across chains**, the deeper the Lore Archive opens.

## The Core Law: Holding is a Key. Bridging is a Pilgrimage.

### 1. Hold = Unlock
Connect a wallet. The site reads which Lil Omnis you own (per chain) and opens
that chapter's riddle trail. One Omni from a realm = that realm's trail is lit.

### 2. Travel = Seek Knowledge
Bridging an Omni to a new chain is not a technical move — it is the Omni
**journeying to a foreign realm to seek knowledge**. Each arrival mints an
"echo" fragment: cross-realm lore that realm's natives have never seen.
An Omni's travel history becomes part of its story (journey stamps in its
Archive page). The oldest, most-traveled pieces become legendary by their
routes alone.

### 3. Depth = Clearance
Tiers, earned by portfolio + travel:

| Tier | Requirement | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| Wanderer | hold 1 Omni | that realm's riddle trail |
| Seeker | hold pieces from 3+ realms | cross-realm contradiction map (the origin theories) |
| Pilgrim | 1 piece that has traveled to 2+ chains | the Echo Chamber — fragments written by travelers |
| Warden | hold all six realms | the Deep Archive: hidden pages, ciphers |
| The Center | the final tier | opens only when Chapter VII (BIG Network) unlocks — the 777 arc |

### 4. Riddle Trails
Each chapter trail has 3 stages:
1. **The Gate** — an open riddle anyone holding the realm can attempt
2. **The Hidden Page** — solvers find an unlinked page (some doors are not
   linked — they are found), containing a cipher
3. **The Sigil Mark** — solving earns a mark on the collector's Archive profile

Riddles are eternal-argument material: per-realm origin theories that
contradict each other. None is ever confirmed (The Unnamed doctrine).

### 5. The 1/111 Souls
Each realm's single Unnamed soul (no sigil, pale eyes) carries a one-shot
riddle. Its holder holds that realm's deepest secret — until they trade it.

## Build Plan (free tools, modular — per project README)
- **Phase 1**: static riddle pages + wallet connect (SIWE-style signed message
  proving ownership) + RPC reads per chain for the collection contract
- **Phase 2**: Archive profile page (holdings, journey stamps, sigil marks,
  tier) rendered from on-chain state
- **Phase 3**: echo fragments minted on bridge arrival (chosen bridge/cross-
  chain messaging at rollout), contradiction map visualization
- **Phase 4**: The Center (Chapter VII) — the final gate, 777
