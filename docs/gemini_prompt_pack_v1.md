# Lil Omnis — Gemini Prompt Pack v1

How to use: attach the base shadow-being image as reference FIRST, then paste the STYLE LOCK + the piece prompt together as one message. Palette picks are opening drafts — BudE can veto/adjust anything.

## STYLE LOCK (paste at the top of every prompt)

> Using the attached image as the exact character reference: keep the same hooded shadow-being - same faceless hood, same painterly brushwork, same proportions, same dark charcoal robe, same crystal-topped staff. Square format. Same art style, texture and lighting as the reference - one more piece from the same collection, not a new style.

## 1) The Six Realm Wardens

**ETHEREUM** — sapphire blue
> Paint him as the Ethereum realm warden: a soft sapphire-blue glow rimming the hood and shoulders, the crystal of his staff glowing sapphire blue, a small glowing blue diamond amulet on a thin dark cord at his chest, faint blue runic embroidery flowing down the front of the robe, and a warm near-black hand-painted chamber behind him with a subtle pool of sapphire light on the floor.

**BASE** — electric azure
> Paint him as the Base realm warden: a bright electric azure glow on the hood and shoulders, the staff crystal glowing azure, a small glowing azure circle-sigil amulet on a thin dark cord, faint azure geometric embroidery flowing down the robe, and a cool near-black chamber with an azure light pool on the floor.

**ROBINHOOD** — emerald
> Paint him as the Robinhood realm warden: an emerald-green glow on the hood and shoulders, the staff crystal glowing emerald, a small gold feather amulet on a thin dark cord, verdant vine-like embroidery flowing down the robe, and a dark chamber with a green light pool on the floor.

**BNB** — golden amber
> Paint him as the BNB realm warden: a warm golden-amber glow on the hood and shoulders, the staff crystal glowing amber, a small glowing amber amulet etched with the BNB mark on a thin dark cord, thin gold-thread embroidery down the robe, and a dark chamber with an amber light pool on the floor.

**SOLANA** — violet
> Paint him as the Solana realm warden: a soft violet glow on the hood and shoulders, the staff crystal glowing iridescent violet, a small violet amulet shaped as three rising bars on a thin dark cord, embroidery of faint intersecting lines of light down the robe, and a dark chamber with a violet light pool on the floor.

**SUI** — liquid cyan
> Paint him as the Sui realm warden: a liquid cyan glow on the hood and shoulders, the staff crystal glowing cyan like water, a small water-drop amulet that seems to hold moving water on a thin dark cord, flowing wave-like cyan embroidery down the robe, and a dark chamber with a rippling cyan pool on the floor.

## 2) Special Characters

**THE UNNAMED** (the 1/1)
> No realm marks at all: no amulet, no embroidery, no colored glow - just the plain charcoal robe and staff with a dim crystal, a faint pale-grey glow deep in the hood, and a plain near-black chamber. The most mysterious piece in the collection.

**THE ASCENDED** (pilgrimage reward — later)
> The same being wearing all six realm pendants across his chest on thin dark cords - sapphire diamond, azure circle, gold feather, amber mark, violet triple-bar, cyan water drop - in that order, with a soft white-gold glow, all six realm patterns woven into one robe, and the six chambers' lights merging into one behind him.
