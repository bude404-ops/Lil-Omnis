## Bridge Marks (earned, additive, never minted)
Bridging your warden to a new chain etches a permanent rune of that realm onto the piece (dynamic):
- 1 bridge: rune etched on the staff shaft
- 2: rune band on the sleeve
- 3: glyph ring faint behind the hood
- 4: second ring + chamber gains a faint second-realm glow accent
- 5: eyes gain a radiant outline
- ALL 6: THE ASCENDED — 1/1. Radiant gold-white version of YOUR warden,
  its realm amulet intact but glowing white, name recorded in the Seeker's Path.
  No new supply: your own token transforms.

## Website: Runes & Riddles (card game sketch)
- 22-card "Seeker's Path" deck: ranks Novice -> Warden as majors, realm wardens as the face cards.
- Cards unlock by solving riddles hidden in lore pages on the site (hidden knowledge theme).
- Solving = lore fragments; fragments reveal trait lore + feed the riddle chain.
- Cards are collectible/tradeable on-site; full deck = role + whitelist tier for future drops.
- Riddle difficulty tiers match Seeker's Path ranks.
